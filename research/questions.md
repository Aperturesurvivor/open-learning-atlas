# Research Questions

## Map Ontology

- Which node distinctions are necessary for a shared educational map?
- Should concept, knowledge, and representation remain top-level kinds or
  become content subtypes?
- When should a mathematical practice be a node, a capability subtype, or an
  evidence dimension?
- What granularity tests remain reliable across mathematical regions?

## Dependency Claims

- Which prerequisites are formal necessities, semantic necessities, empirical
  learning supports, or conventional teaching sequences?
- How should conditional and alternative requirements be normalized?
- What evidence is sufficient to move a relationship from candidate to
  reviewed or accepted?
- How should contradictory but credible claims coexist in a default view?

## Identity And Evolution

- What exact meaning-change test should force a new stable ID?
- How should splits, merges, and narrowed scope migrate old references?
- What record and release formats best support public review at scale?

## Governance And Publication

- Which review roles and thresholds resist institutional or vendor capture?
- Which open-data and software licenses best serve broad educational reuse?
- How should AI-assisted proposals disclose provenance and human
  accountability?
- Which existing ontologies, standards, and knowledge graphs can be aligned
  without importing their course or grade assumptions?

## Downstream Questions, Deferred

Mastery estimation, spaced retention, adaptive practice, project-derived paths,
and curriculum auditing remain important, but they do not determine the v0.1
base-map architecture.
