# Principles

## Make Knowledge Addressable

Every durable entity and relationship needs a stable identity independent of
grades, courses, vendors, and temporary labels.

## Capability Is Central, Not Alone

Observable capabilities are the central educational unit, but they must remain
linked to distinct concepts, knowledge claims, representations, practices, and
composites.

## The Canonical Artifact Is A Claim Graph

Trees and taxonomies are generated views. Relationships are first-class claims
with scope, rationale, provenance, confidence, and review state.

## Open World, Visible Uncertainty

Missing records are not negative claims. Candidate, contested, and deprecated
records remain visible so uncertainty is not converted into false precision.

## Separate Terrain From Routes And Travelers

The base map contains no prescribed curriculum, project, grade placement,
learner state, lesson, test, or mastery policy.

## Preserve Meaning Across Change

Renaming does not change identity. Meaning changes, splits, and merges create
new identities and explicit migration records so old references remain
auditable.

## AI Is Bounded

AI can propose, compare, search, lint, and analyze map records. It must not
silently rewrite accepted meaning or promote its own claims.
