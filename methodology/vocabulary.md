# Vocabulary

- **Base map**: The shared, curriculum-independent graph of educational terrain.
- **Node**: A stable record for a concept, capability, knowledge item,
  representation, practice, or composite.
- **Capability**: A bounded class of observable performance; the central
  educational unit.
- **Concept**: A mathematical or domain object, structure, relation, or idea.
- **Knowledge**: A definition, property, theorem, identity, convention, or
  proposition.
- **Representation**: A form in which mathematical objects or relationships can
  be expressed and interpreted.
- **Practice**: A cross-cutting observable mode of work such as estimating,
  modeling, proving, or inspecting error.
- **Composite**: A non-assessable grouping used to express composition or map
  regions at a larger zoom level.
- **Edge claim**: A stable record asserting a typed relationship between two
  nodes, with rationale, scope, confidence, provenance, and review state.
- **Requirement group**: A conditional all-of, any-of, or at-least dependency
  claim involving multiple assessable nodes.
- **Dependency review**: An open-world marker stating whether a capability's
  prerequisites are unreviewed, partially mapped, or reviewed for completeness
  at its declared scope.
- **Stable ID**: An opaque permanent identifier that survives label and taxonomy
  changes.
- **Record revision**: An immutable version of one node, edge, group, or source.
- **Map release**: An immutable snapshot consumers can pin.
- **View**: A derived tree, hierarchy, filtered graph, or visualization; never
  the canonical source by itself.
- **Overlay**: Downstream data—such as a standard, curriculum, project,
  assessment, institution, or learner model—that references base-map IDs.
