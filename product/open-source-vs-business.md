# Open Infrastructure and Downstream Services

The base map, methodology, release data, validator, and public viewer are open
infrastructure. Their availability must not depend on a proprietary application,
paid account, private API, or one institution.

Different licenses apply by artifact:

- map data and documentation: CC BY 4.0;
- software, schemas, validators, and viewer code: Apache-2.0.

Commercial and nonprofit systems may build hosting, review workflows, content,
assessments, curriculum tools, analytics, tutoring, and learner applications on
top of the atlas. Those systems may remain separate, but they must not redefine
canonical IDs or make proprietary state an intrinsic property of the shared map.

Open Learning Atlas itself does not claim ownership of downstream curricula,
project libraries, assessment instruments, or learner records merely because
they reference atlas IDs.
