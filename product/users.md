# Users

**Status:** deferred downstream product note

The base map is shared infrastructure rather than a learner product. Its future
direct contributors and consumers may include mathematicians, educators,
researchers, standards bodies, assessment designers, curriculum authors,
institutions, AI-system builders, and independent learners.

The earlier app concept targeted:

- homeschool parents
- students with iPads
- families that need low-parent-admin mastery workflows

Possible downstream application users included:

- tutors
- homeschool co-ops
- micro-schools
- private schools
- independent learners
