# Governance

Open Learning Atlas is maintained as shared semantic infrastructure. Governance
must protect stable identity, transparent disagreement, open reuse, and the
separation between the base map and downstream products.

## Roles

### Maintainers

Maintainers merge changes, cut releases, enforce schema and publication rules,
and document governance decisions. Maintainer authority does not make a
mathematical claim true.

### Reviewers

Reviewers evaluate declared dimensions such as mathematical correctness,
educational granularity, accessibility, data integrity, or source quality.
Reviews are attributed and scoped; expertise in one dimension is not implied
to cover every other dimension.

### Contributors

Contributors may propose records, evidence, objections, software, or
documentation. AI systems are tools used by accountable contributors, not
governance participants or authorities.

## Decision Principles

1. Preserve stable identity and public change history.
2. Prefer explicit competing claims over hidden compromise.
3. Separate map inclusion from claim acceptance.
4. Require reasons for semantic changes.
5. Keep the canonical map independent of any curriculum, institution, product,
   or commercial service.
6. Make release and governance decisions inspectable in the repository.

## Initial Governance

Josiah Wilson is the founding maintainer for the initial public-alpha period.
The project should add independent maintainers only after contribution and
review practices have been exercised in public. Governance changes require a
documented proposal and must not retroactively erase attribution or objections.
